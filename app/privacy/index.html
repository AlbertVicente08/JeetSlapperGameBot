Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Jeet Slapper is a free game run by BertoBCN (contact: t.me/RealBertoBcn). This page explains what data the game uses and why. We keep it to the minimum needed to run a fair leaderboard.

What we store

  • Your Telegram account: numeric user id, @username (if you have one) and first name, as sent by Telegram when you open the game.
  • Your games: score, the list of taps (time and square of each tap), the result of the anti-cheat checks and the date.
  • Boosts: whether you are a member of the BertoBCN channel, who invited you (referrals) and how many friends you invited.
  • Wallet (optional): if you link a Phantom wallet, its public address and your $JSLAP tier. We never see or store private keys or seed phrases.

Why

  • To show the leaderboards and the weekly tournament (your name and score are public in them).
  • To detect bots and cheating, and to verify winners before giving prizes.
  • To apply the Community, Squad and Holder boosts.

The legal basis is running the game you choose to play (art. 6.1.b GDPR) and our legitimate interest in keeping it fair (art. 6.1.f GDPR).

Who processes it

  • Supabase (database, EU – Frankfurt) and Cloudflare (hosting).
  • Telegram (to check channel membership and send bot messages).
  • Groq (only the name you or someone else uses with /roast or /slap, to generate the joke).
  • Helius (Solana RPC: only your public wallet address, to read your $JSLAP balance).

We don't sell your data, we don't use advertising trackers and we don't use cookies. The game only stores your local record in your device's storage.

How long

While the game is running. Unused game sessions and anti-cheat logs can be deleted at any time. You can ask us to delete everything about you.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its use, by writing to us in t.me/RealBertoBcn (or DM @albertv_dev on X). If you are in the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

Age

The game is for people aged 16 or over. Anything related to crypto-assets is for adults only (18+).
