Rules & prizes

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Organiser: BertoBCN (t.me/RealBertoBcn)

1. General

  • Jeet Slapper is free to play. Only games played inside Telegram (@JeetSlapperGameBot) count.
  • One person, one Telegram account. Using several accounts, bots, scripts, auto-clickers or modified clients is forbidden.
  • Every game goes through an automatic bot detector. Games that would enter a top 3 are replayed in full on the server. Games that fail don't count.
  • The organiser can review any game and remove scores or disqualify players who cheat. Decisions on cheating are final.

2. Weekly tournament

  • Runs from Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 (UTC). Your best counted game of the week is your score. Boosts don't apply: pure skill.
  • Ties: whoever reached the score first wins.
  • Results are posted every Monday in t.me/RealBertoBcn.
  • Prizes: 🥇 a Telegram Gift + 👑 Hall of Fame badge + shout-out · 🥈 and 🥉 👑 Hall of Fame + shout-out.
  • No purchase is necessary to take part or to win. Holding tokens gives no advantage in the weekly tournament.
  • Winners must have a Telegram account that can receive the prize. Prizes are delivered within 7 days after verification. Unclaimed prizes after 30 days are lost.

3. All-time leaderboard and boosts

  • Your all-time score is your best counted game multiplied by your boosts: 📣 Community x1.5 (member of the channel) · 🤝 Squad +10% per invited friend who played (max +50%) · 👛 Holder x2.5 / x5 (once the token exists).
  • Boosts are re-checked regularly. Leaving the channel or selling tokens removes the boost.
  • Self-referrals and fake accounts don't count and can lead to disqualification.

4. Graduation Jackpot (only if $JSLAP launches and graduates)

  • If $JSLAP is launched on pump.fun and completes its bonding curve (graduates to PumpSwap), the all-time top 20 at that moment share 15,000,000 $JSLAP (1.5% of the supply), held in a public prize wallet.
  • Split: 1st 20% · 2nd 13.3% · 3rd 10% · 4th–10th 5% each · 11th–20th 2.17% each.
  • Paid in 3 parts: 33% at graduation, 33% after 24 h and 34% after 48 h. Each part requires a linked Phantom wallet that still holds the winner's tier. Selling forfeits the remaining parts, which are burned publicly.
  • The prize wallet is a public commitment managed by the organiser (ideally with independent co-signers), not an automatic smart contract.
  • If the token never launches or never graduates, there is no jackpot.

5. Important

  • The game is entertainment. Nothing here is financial advice or a promise of profit.
  • Crypto-assets are extremely volatile and you can lose everything. Never buy what you cannot afford to lose. Anything related to crypto is 18+.
  • Winners are responsible for any taxes that apply to them in their country. Prizes may not be available where the law forbids them.
  • The organiser may change these rules, giving notice in the channel. Changes never apply retroactively to a tournament already running.
