Rules & prizes

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Organiser: BertoBCN (t.me/RealBertoBcn)

1. Eligibility

  • You must be 18 or older to play ranked mode or receive prizes.
  • Prizes are not available where they are prohibited by law. You are responsible for checking that taking part is legal where you live.
  • One person, one Telegram account, one wallet. Multiple accounts, bots, scripts, auto-clickers or modified clients are forbidden.

2. Practice and ranked mode

  • Anyone can play practice mode for free. Practice games don't count.
  • Ranked mode requires playing inside Telegram (@JeetSlapperGameBot) with a linked Phantom wallet (signature only: it costs nothing and never moves funds). Prizes are sent to that wallet.
  • Every ranked game goes through an automatic bot detector. Games that would enter a prize place or a top 3 are replayed in full on the server. Games that fail don't count.
  • The organiser can review any game, remove scores and disqualify players who cheat. Decisions on cheating are final.

3. Boosts

  • Your score in every ranking (weekly, monthly, all-time) is your best counted game multiplied by your boosts:
  • 📣 Community x1.5: member of the channel t.me/RealBertoBcn.
  • 🤝 Squad +10% per invited friend who plays a ranked game (max +50%). Self-referrals and fake accounts don't count.
  • 👛 Holder x2.5 holding ≥ 100,000 $JSLAP, or x5 holding ≥ 500,000 $JSLAP, in the linked wallet, after 30 minutes of continuous holding. Dropping below a tier lowers the boost at once.
  • Boosts are checked when you play and regularly afterwards; the tournament results use the boosts players have when the tournament closes.

4. Tournaments and prizes

  • Weekly: Monday 00:00 → Sunday 23:59 UTC. 🥇 100,000 $JSLAP · 🥈 50,000 $JSLAP · 🥉 25,000 $JSLAP, plus a 👑 Hall of Fame badge.
  • Monthly: calendar month, UTC. 🏆 250,000 $JSLAP for the champion.
  • Ties: whoever reached the score first wins. Results are posted in the channel when each tournament closes.
  • Prizes are sent to the winner's linked wallet within 7 days after verification. If there is no valid wallet, the prize can be claimed for 30 days; after that it is lost.
  • Prize amounts may change for future tournaments (announced in the channel), never for a tournament already running.

5. Graduation Jackpot

  • When $JSLAP completes its pump.fun bonding curve (graduates to PumpSwap), the all-time top 20 at that moment share 15,000,000 $JSLAP (1.5% of the supply), held in a public prize wallet.
  • Split: 1st 20% · 2nd 13.3% · 3rd 10% · 4th–10th 5% each · 11th–20th 2.17% each.
  • Paid in 3 parts: 33% at graduation, 33% after 24 h and 34% after 48 h. Each part requires the winner's wallet to still hold their tier. Selling forfeits the remaining parts, which are burned publicly.
  • The prize wallet is a public commitment managed by the organiser (ideally with independent co-signers), not an automatic smart contract. If the token never graduates, there is no jackpot.

6. Important

  • The game is entertainment. Nothing here is financial advice or a promise of profit. Token prizes can lose all their value.
  • Crypto-assets, and memecoins in particular, are extremely volatile and you can lose everything. Never buy what you cannot afford to lose.
  • Winners are responsible for any taxes that apply to them in their country.
  • The organiser may change these rules, giving notice in the channel. Changes never apply retroactively to a tournament already running.
